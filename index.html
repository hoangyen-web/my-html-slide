Complex Sentences with Adverb Clauses of Time

(Câu phức với mệnh đề trạng ngữ chỉ thời gian)

a. Definition (Định nghĩa)

A complex sentence contains one independent clause and at least one dependent clause.

An adverb clause of time tells when something happens, usually beginning with time connectors like:

before, after, when, while, till/until, as soon as, ...

Example: The roads were slippery when it rained.

b. Common Time Connectors

Connector Meaning Example
When Khi, vào lúc I saw him when I was walking down the street.
While Trong khi Don't use too much water while you are having a shower.
Before Trước khi You must wash your hands before you eat.
After Sau khi We will clean up after we finish our picnic.
As soon as Ngay khi As soon as we arrive, we will visit the park.
Till/Until Cho đến khi We should stay here until the rain stops.

c. Important Notes

1. Clause Position

The adverb clause can come before or after the main clause.

If it comes first, use a comma to separate the clauses.

Correct: When I saw the trash, I picked it up. (comma used)

Correct: I picked up the trash when I saw it. (no comma)

2. Tense Usage

In adverb clauses about the future, use Present Simple, NOT future tense.

Wrong: When I will arrive, I will call you.

Correct: When I arrive, I will call you.

d. Common Mistakes

✗

Forgetting commas: Before you leave turn off the lights.

✓ Before you leave, turn off the lights.

✗

Wrong tense: I will tell him as soon as I will see him.

✓ I will tell him as soon as I see him.
